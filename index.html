Find the life. Then find the home.

Choosing between Cascais, Lisbon and Sintra isn’t simply about finding the right property.

Each offers a completely different way of living. Before we talk about bedrooms, square metres or finishes, I want to understand how you want your everyday life to feel.

Once we know that, we can find the right place to call home.

Cascais

Ocean. Community. Everyday quality of life.

Mornings by the Atlantic, an international community, excellent schools and a relaxed coastal lifestyle, with Lisbon still within easy reach.

Cascais is for those who want life to feel a little more spacious, without feeling disconnected.

Explore Cascais

Lisbon

Energy. Culture. City living.

Historic neighbourhoods, restaurants, culture and the energy of a European capital.

Lisbon offers a completely different rhythm, and choosing the right neighbourhood can change the experience entirely.

Explore Lisbon

Sintra

Nature. Space. Privacy.

Green landscapes, character properties and room to breathe.

Sintra can offer something increasingly difficult to find: privacy and space, while remaining connected to both Cascais and Lisbon.

Client Experience

Good representation sometimes means knowing when to walk away.

A local, by your side.

I’m Natalie Gutman, a real estate advisor based in Cascais, where I’ve lived since 2019.

Before real estate, I worked as a professional commercial buyer, a background that shaped the way I work today: I ask the right questions, pay attention to the details, negotiate carefully and never lose sight of whose interests I represent.

I believe good real estate advice should feel personal. My clients work directly with me, and I stay close to the process from our first conversation to the final signature.

Sometimes that means finding the right opportunity. Sometimes it means negotiating harder. And sometimes it means saying, “This isn’t the right property for you.”

For me, that’s what good representation looks like.
