About

A Tailored Approach to Real Estate Across Cascais, Lisbon & Sintra

Successful real estate acquisition and sale in Portugal rewards deep local integration, data-driven analysis, and precise timing. Based in Cascais since 2019, I offer bespoke property advisory for international clients who expect both deep market knowledge and authentic personal attention.

Drawing from my background as an international commercial buyer, I approach real estate with a clear-eyed evaluation of value and a hard-negotiation mindset. I protect my clients' interests by looking beyond aesthetics to analyze numbers, legal clarity, and long-term yield.

Recognizing that buying or selling abroad requires absolute confidence, I provide a complete end-to-end framework. I work hand-in-hand with leading legal partners, tax advisors, and financial specialists to handle the complex mechanics of every transaction. From initial viewings to notary signatures, I ensure a seamless, discreet, and rewarding experience tailored entirely to your goals.
