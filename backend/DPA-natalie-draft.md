# Data Processing Agreement — DRAFT (not signed, not sent)

Drafted by the in-house `lawyer` agent 2026-07-29 as part of the pre-deploy legal gate.
**Analysis, not legal advice.** Michael reviews and sends; Natalie signs. Placeholders in
`[SQUARE BRACKETS]` must be filled before it goes anywhere.

Why it exists: Natalie is the data controller for her listings; Exclusive Recipe Unipessoal Lda
stores and nightly-backs-up that data on its VPS → processing on her behalf → GDPR Art. 28(3)
requires a written contract. Without it she is exposed on an Art. 28 finding and Michael has no
written liability cap on an unpaid favour.

Two clauses are deliberate and should not be quietly dropped:
- **Clause 4** puts listing accuracy and photo rights on Natalie (she authors the content; we only
  store it). This is the clause that keeps an energy-certificate fine or a copyright claim on her
  side of the line.
- **Clause 12** caps liability at €500 for a free service. Unlimited liability for a favour is a
  bad trade.
