One point of contact throughout your purchase.

A property purchase involves more than choosing a home. There are documents to prepare, professionals to appoint and decisions that need to happen in the right order.

I bring these parts together. That includes helping coordinate your Portuguese tax number (NIF) and bank account setup, introducing you to lawyers, accountants and mortgage specialists where needed, and keeping communication moving between everyone involved.

Buying abroad can bring unfamiliar questions and moments of uncertainty. I take the time to explain, respond and help you find the answers you need. If a question requires input from your lawyer, accountant or bank, I follow up with the right person and keep you informed.

Your appointed professionals advise within their areas of expertise. My role is to coordinate the process, follow up on the practical details and keep you informed as the purchase moves forward.

From your first plans to your new front door.

  1. 01

    Understand your priorities

    We start with your plans, budget and timing. Whether you are relocating, buying a second home or considering an investment, I help you define a clear brief and identify the areas that fit.

  2. 02

    Prepare for the purchase

    I help coordinate the practical preparations, including your NIF and Portuguese bank account, and introduce you to legal, tax and financing professionals according to your needs. We begin these steps early so they can progress alongside the search.

  3. 03

    Search, shortlist and view

    I search across available listings and my professional network, including off-market opportunities where available. I shortlist properties against your brief, arrange viewings and help you assess location, layout, condition and suitability.

  4. 04

    Negotiate and coordinate checks

    When you find a property you want to pursue, I negotiate the proposed price and terms. I coordinate with your lawyer and other appointed professionals so the relevant checks and advice inform your decisions before you commit.

  5. 05

    Move from agreement to completion

    I stay in contact with the agents, lawyer, bank and other parties as the transaction progresses through the promissory contract (CPCV) and final deed. I follow up on outstanding items and keep you informed about the next steps.

  6. 06

    Receive the keys and settle in

    My support continues through the handover, with help coordinating utility transfers and introductions to local service providers where needed, so you can begin making the property your own.

Your questions, answered.

How do we begin if I am still living abroad?

We start with a conversation about your plans, budget and timing. I help you narrow down suitable areas and properties, organise viewings around your visits and arrange video viewings when needed. I also help coordinate the practical preparations so the search and purchase can move forward while you are abroad.

Can you help with a NIF and Portuguese bank account?

Yes. I help coordinate the steps for obtaining your Portuguese tax number (NIF) and opening a Portuguese bank account, including introductions to the relevant providers and follow-up on practical requirements. The appointed provider and bank confirm the documents, eligibility and timing for your circumstances.

Can you introduce me to a lawyer, accountant or mortgage specialist?

Yes. According to your needs, I can introduce you to lawyers, accountants and mortgage specialists and help coordinate communication during the purchase. You appoint your advisers directly, and they provide advice and services within their professional roles.

Does the search include properties beyond your website?

Yes. My search is guided by your brief and can include properties listed by other agents, as well as off-market opportunities where available. The properties on this website are a selection, rather than the full scope of your search.

How do you support me once I have chosen a property?

I negotiate the proposed price and terms, coordinate with your appointed professionals and follow up as the transaction progresses towards completion. You can contact me directly with questions, and I keep you updated on outstanding matters and the next steps through to the handover.

Tell me what you have in mind.

You may have a clear brief or be deciding where to begin. Tell me about your plans, and we can discuss the areas, properties and practical steps relevant to your purchase.
