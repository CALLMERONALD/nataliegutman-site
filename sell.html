Selling your property, end to end.

  1. 01

    Understand & value

    Every sale starts with understanding the property, the market around it and your objectives.

    I assess comparable properties, current competition, location, condition and the characteristics that can influence how buyers perceive value.

    The result is a realistic starting point and a clear strategy for bringing the property to market.

  2. 02

    Prepare

    Before going to market, we make sure the property is ready to be presented properly.

    I coordinate the necessary preparation and help identify anything that should be addressed before launch, from documentation and certification to presentation and professional materials.

    Where additional expertise is required, I connect you with the right professionals.

  3. 03

    Position & present

    A property should not simply be listed. It should be positioned.

    We define how the property should sit within the market, who the likely buyer is and how it should be presented to them.

    Professional photography, property materials and the way the home is communicated are all considered as part of the strategy.

  4. 04

    Market & reach

    The property is introduced through the channels that make sense for the property and the intended buyer.

    This may include the major property platforms, digital marketing, international exposure and direct introductions through my network of agents, buyers and professional contacts.

    The objective is not simply more visibility. It is the right visibility.

  5. 05

    Viewings & feedback

    I manage enquiries and viewings personally and keep you informed throughout the process.

    Buyer feedback matters. It helps us understand how the market is responding and, when necessary, make informed decisions rather than reacting blindly.

    You know what is happening, what buyers are saying and what I recommend we do next.

  6. 06

    Offers & negotiation

    Negotiation with perspective.

    My background as a professional commercial buyer gives me an understanding of how the other side of a negotiation thinks.

    I assess offers beyond the headline number, looking at the buyer, their position, conditions, timing and likelihood of completion.

    I use that perspective to protect your position and negotiate the strongest possible terms.

  7. 07

    From agreement to completion

    Once an offer is accepted, I remain involved.

    I coordinate with the lawyers and other professionals involved, follow the transaction through its different stages and help keep the process moving toward completion.

    My role does not end when a buyer says yes. I stay with you until the transaction is complete.

Discretion when it matters.

Not every property needs the same exposure.

Some properties benefit from broad market visibility. Others require a more discreet approach.

Depending on the property and your objectives, we can define the appropriate strategy, from full-market exposure to a more targeted introduction through selected buyers and professional networks.

The approach should fit the property, not the other way around.

Common questions

Selling in Portugal, answered.

How do you determine the value of my property?

I look beyond the asking prices of similar properties.

Location, condition, layout, views, outdoor space, recent comparable transactions, current competition and buyer demand all influence how a property should be positioned.

The objective is not to give you the highest number. It is to give you an informed and realistic view of where your property sits in the current market.

What costs should I expect when selling a property in Portugal?

The costs depend on the property and your individual circumstances, but may include agency fees, required documentation or certification, legal costs and potential tax obligations.

Before proceeding, I can help you understand which professionals you should speak with so that you have a clear picture of the transaction.

Do I need an Energy Certificate to sell?

An Energy Performance Certificate is generally required when marketing and selling property in Portugal, subject to the applicable rules and any exemptions.

If you do not already have one, I can help coordinate the process with the appropriate professional.

Will I have to pay capital gains tax?

Capital gains treatment depends on factors including your tax residency, how the property has been used and your individual circumstances.

Because tax treatment is specific to each seller, I recommend confirming your position with a qualified tax adviser or accountant. I can connect you with the appropriate professional if needed.

Can you manage the sale if I am not in Portugal?

Yes.

Many owners are not permanently based in Portugal. I can manage the property marketing, enquiries, viewings and communication locally while coordinating with you and the professionals involved in the transaction.

The objective is to make the process clear and manageable wherever you are based.
