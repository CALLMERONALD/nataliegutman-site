# Photo credits & licence provenance

Where the site's stock photography comes from and under what licence. Keep this current —
an unlicensed image on a client site is a real liability, and "we can't remember where it
came from" is not an answer.

## Prime Locations (index.html)

Replaced 2026-08-04. The previous three were portrait 1200×1607 images inside a landscape
4:3 frame, so `object-cover` was discarding **44%** of every photo. These are pre-cropped to
exactly 4:3 (1200×900), so nothing is cropped away at any breakpoint.

| File | Subject | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cascais.jpg` | Cascais bay, fishing pier, hillside town | Unsplash photo `1587973878097-ef3aabd5eeaa` | [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) — free for commercial use, no attribution required |
| `lisbon.jpg` | Alfama rooftops, São Vicente de Fora + Panteão Nacional | Unsplash photo `1501927023255-9063be98970c` | [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) |
| `sintra.jpg` | Pena Palace above the forested hills | Unsplash photo `1697050303652-0b228f3f83df` | [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) |

Each image was **visually verified to be the city it is labelled as** before use. This matters:
Unsplash location tags are unreliable — a "Cascais" search returned a photo of Porto's Ribeira,
and a "Lisbon" search returned the Golden Gate Bridge. Publishing the wrong city on a property
site is a misleading commercial practice (DL 57/2008), so **never trust the caption; look at the
photo.**

Originals of the replaced images are not in the repo. If they are ever wanted back, they were
the golden-hour portrait shots used from 2026-06-18 to 2026-08-04 (recoverable from git history).

## Property photography

Listing photographs (`assets/photos/gandarinha*`, `assets/walkthrough/`, and everything uploaded
through the admin into the `natalie-properties` bucket) are **not** stock. They come from the
seller/developer and are covered by the permission recorded in `ISSUES.md`. Do not add property
imagery here without a written permission record.
