The residence

Where Atlantic light meets architectural mastery

Set behind private gates in Cascais' most prestigious enclave, the estate offers a rare balance of absolute privacy, grand scale and modern architecture. Every living space is framed by expansive floor-to-ceiling glass, allowing the interior to flow naturally into lush private gardens, limestone terraces and the grand 15.5-metre swimming pool.

A rare opportunity in Gandarinha: properties of this magnitude on single private parcels rarely come to market here. It represents an exceptional and exclusive offering in Cascais' most tightly held residential enclave.

Cascais

The lifestyle: where sea meets elegance

Cascais is a historic, charming coastal town that offers an unparalleled quality of life, thirty minutes from Lisbon. With over 300 sunny days a year, it is a haven of natural beauty, golf and culture.

Championship golf

Home to five world-class golf courses, including Quinta da Marinha and Oitavos Dunes.

Gastronomy & culture

Michelin-starred dining at Fortaleza do Guincho, local seafood, and cultural landmarks like Casa das Histórias Paula Rego.

Active coastal living

Miles of seaside bicycle paths, scenic views of Cabo da Roca, and world-renowned surf beaches like Guincho.

The surroundings

Everything Cascais is minutes away
