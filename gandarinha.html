A Rare Address in Cascais

Gandarinha occupies a particularly privileged position within Cascais: close enough to the town’s waterfront, marina and historic centre to make everyday life effortless, yet sufficiently discreet to preserve a sense of privacy.

Properties offering this combination of scale, privacy and proximity to the Atlantic are rarely offered to the market.

Here, the address is not simply part of the residence. It is part of the opportunity.

The Residence

Set within one of Cascais’ most sought-after residential enclaves, the residence is conceived around space, light and an effortless relationship between architecture and landscape.

Generous living areas open directly towards the gardens, terraces and pool, creating a natural flow between quiet private moments and larger gatherings.

Across three levels, the residence balances entertaining spaces, private bedroom suites and discreet service areas, while a private lift connects the home throughout.

The result is a residence that feels substantial without becoming formal, private without feeling isolated.
