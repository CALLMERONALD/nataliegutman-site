A rare residence in the heart of Gandarinha

Set within Gandarinha, one of the most prestigious and sought-after residential enclaves in Cascais, this exceptional villa offers a rare sense of space, privacy, and effortless comfort.

Properties of this calibre are seldom offered for sale in Gandarinha, making the location itself part of the home's appeal. Quiet and discreet, yet ideally positioned close to the centre of Cascais, the coastline, beaches, restaurants, and all essential amenities, it is a setting that offers both seclusion and convenience.

The residence is defined by generous proportions and a refined sense of comfort. Expansive living spaces, carefully selected materials, elegant finishes, and a seamless relationship with the private gardens and impressive swimming pool create an atmosphere that feels both sophisticated and deeply inviting.

Thoughtful features such as a private lift and underfloor heating add to the everyday experience, while the scale and quality of the property allow it to be enjoyed equally as a peaceful private home or as a place to gather and entertain.

A truly uncommon opportunity to acquire a substantial private residence in one of Cascais' most tightly held addresses, where exceptional homes rarely come to market.

Where the sea meets a refined way of life

Cascais offers a rare balance of coastal beauty, understated elegance and an exceptional quality of life. Just 30 minutes from Lisbon, the town combines the ease of seaside living with excellent dining, culture, golf and nature, all within easy reach.

World-class golf

Some of Portugal's finest golf courses are close by, including Quinta da Marinha and Oitavos Dunes, offering exceptional courses set among the Atlantic landscape.

Dining & culture

From refined seafood and acclaimed restaurants to galleries, museums and a vibrant cultural scene, Cascais offers an effortless blend of gastronomy, art and local character.

Life by the Atlantic

The coastline is part of everyday life. Scenic cycling and walking routes, dramatic Atlantic landscapes and beaches such as Guincho are all within easy reach, offering a natural extension of the Cascais lifestyle.

Prime location & surroundings

Beaches, the marina, the golf courses and Cabo da Roca surround the estate — with Lisbon thirty minutes away via the coast road. Touch a landmark to place it on the coast.
